# Scope

This Working Group develops the FPSOT applications specification.

The specification covers software that takes in an application to a public body, in any domain and any channel, from the first draft to the decision or the withdrawal: jurisdiction profiles of intake law with their sources, calendars, language rules and prefill bases; service declarations of programs; form definitions as versioned JSON Schema documents with stable question ids, purposes, prefill rules and languages; the lifecycle of an application file, drafts and their expiry, explicit migrations to new form versions, submission and immutable file versions; receipt records, acknowledgments and reference numbers; requests for information, clocks, deadlines and outage windows; attachments, their digests, scans, versions and archival renditions; representation and the record of the acting party; the status that the applicant sees, notifications and events; assisted, paper and digital channels and idempotent submission; the interface of an intake service, the handoff to fees, registries and decisions, and the fixtures that test them. It includes a LinkML model, jurisdiction profiles, event schemas, example files and the scripts that check them.

It does not cover form builders, rule builders or flow builders; the processing of a case; the decision and its notice; fees; identity proofing; the records schedule of a closed file; time, parties, decision records, seals, audit events, APIs, authorization and accessibility, which other FPSOT specifications set; the choice of a database, an engine or a product; or legal advice.

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
