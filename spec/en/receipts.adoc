= Receipts and acknowledgments
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A deadline often turns on when an application arrived.
Québec presumes that an electronic document arrives when it becomes accessible at the address that the body names.
It accepts as proof stored data that fix the date, hour, minute and second, with the origin and the destination.
France asks each body to acknowledge each application, and names what the acknowledgment holds.
Canada leaves both points to each program.

This page therefore records each arrival in the same way, and lets the profile say what that record proves.
The receipt record here differs from the receipt of a fee: it proves an arrival, not a payment.

== Receipt record

[[APP-040]]
*APP-040.* Each submission MUST create one record of the class `ReceiptRecord` of `model/applications.yaml`.
That includes the first submission and each reply to a request for information.
The record MUST NOT change once created.
It MUST hold these parts.

* The file, the file version and the reference number.
* The received instant in UTC, to the second, under DAT-010, and its precision under APP-041.
* The local date and time of receipt with the legal time zone of the profile, under DAT-015.
* The channel, the submitter and any representative, under APP-071, and the destination, such as the address or the office.
* The SHA-256 digests of the form version used, of the answers and of each attachment, under APP-062.
* The digest of the archival rendition that APP-065 asks for.

The local time with its zone matters where a law reads deadlines in local time.
The federal Interpretation Act reads a time of day as standard time, and the profile says how to show it.

[[APP-041]]
*APP-041.* The received instant MUST be the instant when the submission became accessible in the system of the body.
It MUST NOT be the instant when a person or a process first opened, checked or processed it.
For paper, it MUST be the instant that the body stamped on the document when it arrived, under APP-092.
The received instant MUST stay the same when the file moves to another channel or another system.
The record MUST state its precision in `receivedPrecision`, from this list.

* `day` for a stamp with a date only, with the start of that local day in the legal time zone.
* `minute` for a stamp to the minute, with the start of that minute.
* `second` for every other receipt.

A queue, a backlog or a migration then never changes the age of a file.
A date stamp counts from its own date, and no body invents an hour for it.
The profile states what the received instant proves in its law.

[[APP-042]]
*APP-042.* The {term-deploying-body} MUST seal each receipt record under the signed records specification, in a format of LOG-010.
The id of the receipt record MUST be the id of its seal manifest, under LOG-019.
The seal MUST cover the receipt record and the copy of the form version of APP-036.
The seal profile MUST be `seal-log` or higher, under LOG-002.
A correction MUST be a new receipt record that names the one that it corrects, under LOG-082.

The time stamp of the seal shows when the record existed, under LOG-022.
The log then proves that nobody moved the received instant later.

== Acknowledgment

[[APP-043]]
*APP-043.* The service MUST send an `Acknowledgment` for each submission, in the language of the file.
Each acknowledgment MUST hold these parts.

* The reference number and the received instant, shown in the legal time zone.
* The body that handles the application, with a way to contact it.
* The expected date of a decision, or the service standard of the program.
* Each missing item with its deadline, when the service already knows of one.
* How to see the status of the application.

A profile MAY add parts, such as the effect of silence or the routes of appeal that France asks for.

An email that says "received" cuts calls from people who wonder whether their application arrived.
The acknowledgment holds no answer and no attachment, under APP-085.

[[APP-044]]
*APP-044.* A digital channel SHOULD show the acknowledgment at once, on the confirmation page.
When the service cannot acknowledge at once, it MUST send at once a notice of registration.
That notice holds the reference number and the received instant.
It MUST then send the acknowledgment within the period that the profile sets.
The confirmation page MUST offer a copy of the submission to keep, such as its archival rendition.
A return to the confirmation page MUST NOT submit again.

France asks for a notice of registration within one working day when the full acknowledgment cannot come at once.
The model of this rule comes from there, and the period comes from each profile.

== Reference numbers

[[APP-045]]
*APP-045.* Each file MUST have a reference number of eight random characters and one check character.
A random source fit for cryptography MUST draw the eight characters from the Base32 alphabet of Crockford.
The check character MUST be the value of the eight characters modulo 37, in the check symbols of that scheme.
The reference MUST appear as `XXXX-XXXX-C`, in capitals.
It MUST NOT follow a sequence, and MUST NOT encode a person, a place, a program or a subject.

That alphabet holds the digits and the letters except I, L, O and U.
DAT-062 already bars an identifier that tells something about a person, a place or a business.

The reference is like a bearer token, so a sequence would let someone guess the files of others.
Eight characters give about a trillion values, and the check character catches a wrong character or two swapped ones.
The check symbols include `*`, `~`, `$`, `=` and `U`.
A front end therefore shows the reference in a monospace font.
A web address percent-encodes those symbols, and staff spell them out on the phone.

[[APP-046]]
*APP-046.* A service MUST read a reference without regard to case, spaces and hyphens.
It MUST read I and L as 1, and O as 0.
It MUST refuse a reference whose check character does not match, and say that it may hold a typo.
A lookup of status by reference MUST also ask for a second factor, such as a sign-in.
Its answer MUST be the same whether or not the reference exists.

A typo then never shows the file of another person.
The same answer for a missing reference stops a person from testing references one by one.
