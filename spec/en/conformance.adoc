= Interfaces, handoffs and conformance
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

A body may change its portal, its case system or its fee engine, and keep its files.
This page sets the interface of an intake service and the handoff to the other specifications.
It also sets the fixtures that prove both.

== Interfaces

[[APP-100]]
*APP-100.* An interface MUST create drafts, submissions, replies and withdrawals with `POST`.
It MUST accept an `Idempotency-Key` on each, under API-060.
It MUST NOT offer an operation that edits or deletes a submitted file version or a receipt record.
The same holds for the content of a request and for an acknowledgment.
A withdrawal or a change of status of a request MUST be a new resource, never a `DELETE`.
The interface MUST ask the authorization engine on every request, under IAM-050.

A withdrawal as a resource keeps its own id, its instant and its actor.
A `DELETE` would erase that trace.

== Handoffs

[[APP-101]]
*APP-101.* A file MUST record in `handoffs` each record that it starts in another specification, by kind and id.
A submission MAY start a fee order under FEE-040, a filing under REG-020 to REG-024, or a decision under DEC-040.
The intake service MUST NOT set the payment status of a fee order or the status of a filing.
It MUST NOT set the result of a decision.
It reads them from their owners.

The pleasure craft example starts both a fee order and a filing from one submission.
The fee engine then owns the payment, and the register owns the license.

== Fixtures

[[APP-102]]
*APP-102.* A conformance suite of this specification MUST hold forms and service declarations.
It MUST hold files with the versions, receipt records and requests that belong to them.
It MUST also hold scenarios in English and in French, under TST-020 to TST-036.
Each scenario MUST state its dates and name its states.
A producer SHOULD test each event against the schema of its type, under TST-043.

The folder `examples/` holds the first such fixtures, and `examples/test-examples.sh` checks that the check rejects broken copies.

[[APP-103]]
*APP-103.* Fixtures, examples and tests MUST use synthetic data only, under TST-050.
Parties, accounts, reference numbers and attachments MUST be fictional.
Domains MUST be those that standards reserve for documentation, such as `example.org` or a name under `.example`.

[[APP-104]]
*APP-104.* A service MAY offer an adapter to the Registration building block of GovStack.
Such an adapter MUST map each requirement that it claims to a rule of this specification.

[[APP-105]]
*APP-105.* A service or a front end that replaces another one MUST pass the same conformance suite, under TST-045.

A body can then change its supplier without a change to its files.
