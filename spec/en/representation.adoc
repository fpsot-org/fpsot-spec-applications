= Representation
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

Many applications come from someone other than the applicant.
It may be a parent, a mandatary, an accountant or the officer of a business.
The identity and access specification already sets who may act for whom, under IAM-060 to IAM-064.
Démarche Numérique accepts a file for another person on a declaration alone.
The Dutch case APIs record the authorized party and the principal as two roles on the case.
This page checks the authority on every act, and records the two parties apart.

== Authority

[[APP-070]]
*APP-070.* An act of a representative MUST rest on a representation under IAM-061, chosen in the session under IAM-064.
The service MUST ask the {term-authorization-engine} on each act of an applicant, a representative or staff, under IAM-050.
That covers each save of a draft, each submission, each reply, each withdrawal and each lookup.
The service MUST record when it checked each act, and MUST refuse an act that fails the check.
It MUST refuse an act of a representative when no valid representation covers it.
A representative MUST act from their own account, under IAM-060, never from the account of the represented party.

A representation can end between the draft and the reply.
A check on each act then stops a person whose mandate ended.
Staff act on the authority of their role under IAM-050, not on a representation.
The same check applies to them.

[[APP-071]]
*APP-071.* Each act on a file MUST record an `ActingParty` that keeps the acting party and the subject apart.
It holds the account of the actor by an opaque id.
It holds the party who acts and the party whose file it is, both under DMO-040.
It also holds the role of the actor, the representation by id and the instant of the check.
A staff member who keys in an application MUST appear as the actor, with the role `staff`.

The file then answers two questions for each act: who did it, and for whom.
FHIR keeps the same split between the subject of a response, its source and its author.

[[APP-072]]
*APP-072.* A service MAY accept a submission that a person declares for another party, without a representation under IAM-061.
It MUST do so only when its declaration allows it, and then these hold.

* The service MUST mark the file, each act and the receipt record `self-declared`.
* Staff MUST see that mark.
* Only the draft and its submission MAY be self-declared.
* A later act, such as a reply, a withdrawal or a lookup, MUST come from the subject or a representative.
* The submission MUST NOT give the declarant any later access to the file.
* A notice to the declarant MUST only confirm the submission, with no link to the file.

A person who helps a neighbor once can then still submit, and the body knows how much to trust it.
The subject learns of the submission under APP-073, and decides what happens next.

== Notice and revocation

[[APP-073]]
*APP-073.* When a person submits for another party, the service MUST notify the represented party of the submission.
It MAY skip the notice when no contact is on record.
It MAY also skip it when the profile or a court order says that it puts a person at risk.
It MUST then record the reason.

A notice tells a person that someone applied in their name, and lets them object.

[[APP-074]]
*APP-074.* A revoked or ended representation MUST block each new act that rests on it.
It MUST NOT erase or change the acts done before.
The represented party MUST be able to see, on their own file, who acted for them and when.
