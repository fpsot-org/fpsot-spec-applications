= Questions for counsel
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This page is informative.
It sets no rule, and it is not legal advice.
It lists the legal questions that this specification raises but cannot answer.
A lawyer must answer each one before {abbr-fpsot-first} or a body that takes in applications relies on it.

The research read the federal texts at the Justice Laws Website.
It read the Québec texts at LégisQuébec, current to 2026-10-15.
It read the French code in archived copies of 2024 and 2025 only.
A point marked _to be verified_ rests on a secondary source, a search result or an old archived copy.

== Time of receipt

. *Federal receipt time.* The federal law on electronic documents sets no default time of receipt.
Its section 50 lets the authority responsible for a provision listed in Schedule 2 or 3 set one by regulation.
For other provisions, and when such regulations are silent, when does an online application count as filed?
May a department fix that time in the published terms of use of its portal?

. *Standard time.* Section 29 of the federal Interpretation Act reads a time of day as standard time.
In summer, does a deadline at 23:59 mean 23:59 standard time, which is 00:59 local time?
How should a service show such a deadline to the applicant?

. *An address that is down.* Article 31 of the Québec law on information technology presumes receipt at an active address.
If the portal is down at the deadline, is its address still active?
Does a corrupt attachment rebut the presumption that the document is intelligible?
What notice must the body then send?

== Drafts and withdrawal

[start=4]
. *Abandoned drafts, federal.* Under the Privacy Act, does a portal hold the data of a draft for an administrative purpose?
If so, the floor of two years of the Privacy Regulations would apply.
May a service purge a draft sooner?

. *Abandoned drafts, Québec.* Must the retention schedule under the Québec archives law cover a draft that nobody submitted?
Or may the body destroy it under article 73 of the access law once the applicant leaves it?

. *Withdrawal.* Does a withdrawal end the processing at once, in each jurisdiction?
May the body still decide, such as on a fraud?
Must the body keep a withdrawn file as long as a submitted one?

== Language

[start=7]
. *Exceptions under article 21.9.* A legal person or an enterprise seeks a permit or a grant in French only.
Article 21.9 applies the exceptions of articles 21.4 and 21.6, adapted, and allows others by regulation.
Which applicants may then join a version in another language, or write in another language only?
Does article 21.9 reach the foreign certificates and technical reports that an applicant attaches?
Must a service refuse such an application in another language, or accept it and ask for French?

. *English under articles 22.2 and 22.3.* How should a body check that a person is eligible for English instruction?
How should it record that fact, and may it rely on the statement of the applicant?
Does an English user interface for a form need a basis under article 22.3?

== Prefill

[start=9]
. *Federal prefill.* May a federal form fill answers from another institution on consent shown in the form?
Or does it need one of the grounds of disclosure of section 8 of the Privacy Act?
Does section 5 of that Act bar prefill by default?

. *Québec prefill.* Under the law on information resources, a decree can name the official sources of some data.
Outside those sources, may a body prefill from a partner body?
Does it need a separate agreement for each program under article 64 of the access law?

== Representation and requests

[start=11]
. *Apparent mandate.* Article 2163 of the Civil Code of Québec binds a person who let another appear as their mandatary.
The mandate may turn out not to exist.
What check of the person who submits then protects the body and the represented party?

. *The time that is needed.* Article 6 of the Act respecting administrative justice speaks of the time that is needed.
The body delays an unfavorable decision for that time.
May a service set a default period for a request for information, such as 30 days?
Would that breach the duty to act with fairness?

. *France.* The archived copies of the code on relations between the public and the administration date from 2024 and 2025.
Have its article L231-1, or the decrees of exceptions, changed since?
The live text at Légifrance is _to be verified_.

. *Saturday in Québec.* Article 52 of the Québec Interpretation Act moves a time limit to the next working day.
It names Saturday only for the land register.
Is a Saturday a working day for other time limits?
The `ca-qc` profile skips Saturdays until counsel answers.

. *Holidays of a place.* Section 35 of the federal Interpretation Act also counts provincial and civic holidays.
For a federal time limit, whose place counts: the body, the office that handles the file, or the applicant?
Which acts of a province make a day non-juridical there, and do they include Saturdays?
The `ca-federal` profile marks its national calendar incomplete until counsel answers.
