= Channels
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

In 2022, the passport office of the United Kingdom moved 134,000 applications from its digital system to paper.
Its National Audit Office found that staff could not tell when a case had moved.
Some applications then showed the wrong age.
A federal audit of 2026 found that only 41% of the reviewed counter applications had no error.
Denmark makes many services digital by default, and must still offer another route to people who cannot use them.
This page makes every channel feed the same file, with the same rules.

== One file for every channel

[[APP-090]]
*APP-090.* Paper, telephone, counter and digital intake MUST create or update the same kind of file, with the same lifecycle.
They MUST use the same form version, the same checks of answers and the same checks of attachments.
A staff screen MUST NOT accept an answer that the digital channel would refuse.

Validation at the point of capture is what keeps assisted intake from adding errors.

[[APP-091]]
*APP-091.* Each file version MUST record its `channel`: `digital`, `assisted-digital`, `counter`, `telephone`, `paper` or `email`.
For assisted entry, it MUST record in `capturedBy` the staff member who keyed in the answers.
That staff member also appears as the actor under APP-071.

[[APP-092]]
*APP-092.* For paper and for documents brought to a counter, the received instant MUST be the stamp on arrival.
The record gives that stamp to the second, the minute or the day, under APP-041.
The service MUST record the instant of keying apart, in `keyedAt`.
The first version of a paper application MUST hold its original or a scan with its digest as an attachment.

A backlog in the mailroom then never shortens the time of the applicant.

[[APP-093]]
*APP-093.* A service that is digital by default MUST offer a route that is not digital to the same file.
A service MAY offer only a digital route when its profile cites a law that allows it.
The service declaration then names that source.

Québec leaves the choice of medium to the person when a service exists on several media.
In France, a body may reserve an online service for a procedure.
That service is then the only electronic route for that procedure.
That rule says nothing of paper, and the comparison data says so.

== Repeated submits

[[APP-094]]
*APP-094.* Submitting a draft MUST be idempotent.
A repeated submit of the same draft MUST return the existing reference number and receipt record.
It MUST NOT create a second file.
An interface MUST accept an `Idempotency-Key` on submit, under API-060 to API-063.
It MUST refuse a key that comes back with a different body.

A double click, a timeout or the back button then never creates two files.
GOV.UK reminds teams that a button that blocks a double click works in the browser only.
The server must also guard.

[[APP-095]]
*APP-095.* A service SHOULD flag for staff a second open file of the same subject for the same program.
It MUST NOT merge, drop or refuse that file on its own.

An applicant and a representative can both submit.
Only a person can tell a duplicate from a second real need.

== Outages

[[APP-096]]
*APP-096.* During an outage of a digital channel, the operator MUST accept the same applications through another channel.
That holds unless the service is digital only under APP-093.
Such a service applies the extension rule of its program instead, under APP-056.
The received instant then follows APP-041.
A page of error or outage MUST say whether the service kept the answers in progress, and until when.
It MUST also say when the service expects to return, and what happens to deadlines.

The record of the outage itself comes under APP-056.
