= Requests for information and clocks
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

Most files lack something at some point.
Québec gives the applicant the chance to complete the file.
It delays an unfavorable decision on a benefit until the applicant could do so.
France lists the missing items in a letter with a deadline.
That letter stops or suspends the clocks of silence.
Canada leaves both points to each program and its service standard.

Démarche Numérique turns this into data.
A request there is a message with a reason, and at most one is open at a time.
GovStack lets staff mark the field or the document that is wrong.
This page joins the two, and keeps each clock and each calendar in data.

== Requests

[[APP-050]]
*APP-050.* Each request for information MUST be a record of the class `InformationRequest` of `model/applications.yaml`.
It MUST hold these parts.

* A reason: `incorrect`, `incomplete`, `outdated` or `complement`.
* The items that it concerns, each a question `id` or an attachment that the file version holds.
* A deadline, computed under APP-055.
* Its effect on each clock of the program: `start-on-completion`, `suspend` or `none`, under APP-054.
* The acting party who opened it, under APP-071.

`complement` means that the body added something and waits for the applicant to confirm it.
The items tell the applicant what to fix, and the front end shows them in place.

[[APP-051]]
*APP-051.* At most one request for information MAY be open on a file at a time.
Opening a request MUST move the file to `information-requested`, under APP-031.
A request MUST ask only for items that the form or the rules of the program need.
A request MUST have one status: `open`, `resolved`, `cancelled` or `expired`.

One open request gives the applicant one list and one deadline.
France forbids a body to ask for items that no law requires, and the French comparison says so.

[[APP-052]]
*APP-052.* While a request is open and in time, a file MUST NOT move to `decided` on an adverse outcome.
The service MUST tell the decision service that the request is open.
A missing fact then leads to `insufficient-information` or `referred`, under DEC-025.
A favorable decision MAY still go ahead when the program allows it.

Québec asks the body to delay an unfavorable decision on a benefit while the applicant completes the file.
France lets the grant of a right go ahead while a document is missing, with effect once it arrives.
This rule holds the first everywhere, and lets the program allow the second.

[[APP-053]]
*APP-053.* A reply MUST add a new `FileVersion` and a new receipt record, under APP-037 and APP-040.
A new file for an item MUST be a new attachment version, under APP-064.
The reply MUST resolve the request, which records the instant and the new version.
A body MAY cancel a request, with the instant and a reason code.
The front end MUST fill the reply with the answers already given, under APP-024.

The reply then has its own received instant, and the first submission keeps its own.

== Clocks

[[APP-054]]
*APP-054.* Each service declaration MUST list the clocks of its program, such as a service standard.
Each clock MUST state its code, its kind and when it starts: on submission or on a complete file.
Each change to a clock MUST be an entry of the file, with the instant.
The change is `started`, `suspended`, `resumed` or `stopped`.
The service MUST apply the effect of each request on each clock from data, and MUST NOT code it.

A service standard and a statutory time limit can react in two ways to the same request.
France starts the clock of silence that means acceptance once the file is complete.
It suspends the clock of silence that means refusal.

[[APP-055]]
*APP-055.* A service MUST compute each deadline from a `CalendarRule` of the profile.
The rule names the legal time zone and the holidays with their dates.
It also names the way to roll a deadline that falls on a holiday.
The record of the deadline MUST name the calendar and its version, the start and the period.
It MUST also hold the raw date and the date after the roll.
A service MUST NOT hard-code a calendar or a holiday.

The federal and Québec lists of holidays differ.
Québec has 24 June, and the federal list has 30 September and 11 November.
A deadline that falls on 24 June then rolls in Québec, and not in a federal program.

[[APP-056]]
*APP-056.* A service MUST record each outage as an `OutageWindow`, with its start, its end and its channels.
It MUST publish each window to the applicants of the program.
A service declaration MAY name an extension rule of its program for a deadline that falls in an outage.
The service MUST apply that rule from data, and record on the deadline the window and the rule that applied.

This specification owns the record of an outage, not the policy of an extension.
A program that extends a deadline after an outage can then show why the date moved.

== Closing for no response

[[APP-057]]
*APP-057.* A service MAY move a file to `closed` only when all of these hold.

* The service declaration allows closing for no response.
* The deadline of the open request has passed, and the request is `expired`.
* The file holds a logged contact attempt after the request opened, through a channel that did not fail, under APP-087.

A file never closes for silence while the applicant might not know that the body asked.
The front end tells the applicant before a file closes, under APP-081.
