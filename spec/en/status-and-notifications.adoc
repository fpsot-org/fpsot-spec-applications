= Status and notifications
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

The federal Directive on Service and Digital asks new online services to show the status of applications in real time.
The tracker of the federal immigration department shows three overall states.
One of its section states asks the applicant to act.
Its closed state says nothing about the outcome, and the decision comes in its own letter.
GOV.UK leaves sensitive data out of emails, and links only to its own domain.
This page takes those lessons, and sets the events that other systems read.

== The status that the applicant sees

[[APP-080]]
*APP-080.* The status that an applicant sees MUST be a `StatusProjection`, with a label from this closed list.
Each state of a file MUST map to one label, as the table shows.
The service MUST publish the labels and their translations.

[cols="1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
|State of the file |Label |Text in English and French
|`draft` |`not-sent` |Not sent, Non transmise
|`submitted` |`received` |Received, Reçue
|`in-review` |`in-progress` |In progress, En traitement
|`information-requested` |`action-needed` |Action needed, Action requise
|`decided` |`decision-made` |Decision made, Décision rendue
|`withdrawn` |`withdrawn` |Withdrawn, Retirée
|`closed` |`closed` |Closed, Fermée
|===

A small closed list is what people can learn.
Each internal step of a review shows as `in-progress`, so the label changes only at the moments that matter.

[[APP-081]]
*APP-081.* `action-needed` MUST be the only label that asks the applicant to act.
With it, the projection MUST show what is needed, by when, and how to respond.
The items MUST be those of the open request.
The deadline MUST be the deadline of that request, in the legal time zone.

[[APP-082]]
*APP-082.* A projection MUST NOT show a queue, a sub-state, a risk flag, a score or a staff name.
A label MUST NOT show the outcome of a decision.
The decision reaches the applicant in its notice, under DEC-057.
A projection MUST show the reference number, the received instant and the body that handles the file.
It MUST also show the expected date or the service standard.

A label on a shared screen then tells nobody whether the answer was yes or no.

[[APP-083]]
*APP-083.* The service MUST derive the projection from the file of record on each read.
It MAY instead rebuild the projection in the same transaction as each change, staff changes included.
A projection MUST NOT keep a copy of data that can drift from the file.

People call when a tracking page shows an old date of birth or an old email address.

== Notifications

[[APP-084]]
*APP-084.* The service MUST notify the applicant of each submission, through a channel that the applicant chose.
It MUST notify the applicant when a request opens, and record that notice with the request, under APP-087.
It SHOULD notify the applicant again before the deadline of the request.
It SHOULD also do so when a decision is available, and before it purges a draft.
It SHOULD NOT send one for a change that keeps the same label.

Receipt is itself the most useful news.
A change of internal step is noise, and leads to calls.

[[APP-085]]
*APP-085.* A notification MUST NOT hold the content of a decision, its reasons, an answer or any sensitive data.
It MUST NOT ask for personal information, and MUST NOT carry an attachment.
It MUST say where the applicant can read the content, such as a signed-in page or an official notice.

Text messages travel without encryption, and some emails do too.
A notification therefore says that something changed and where to look, and nothing more.
A name next to a program on a locked phone tells others that a person applied.
So a notification names neither.

[[APP-086]]
*APP-086.* Each link in a notification MUST point to a domain of the body, written in full.
It MUST NOT go through a redirect or carry a tracking parameter.
Each notification SHOULD come from a sender address or number that the body publishes.

A person can then check where a link goes before they follow it.
A known sender and a full domain make phishing harder, without any personal data in the message.

[[APP-087]]
*APP-087.* The service MUST record each notification on the file, with its kind, channel, instant and delivery status.
It MUST record each delivery failure that the channel reports.
After a failure, the service SHOULD try another channel on record, such as post or the representative.

The record of delivery is what shows, under APP-057, that the body tried to reach the applicant.

== Events

[[APP-088]]
*APP-088.* The service MUST send the events of this table as CloudEvents, under API-100 to API-104.
The data of each event type MUST follow its JSON Schema in `registry/events/`, under API-103.
The `subject` of each event MUST be the id of the file, even for a draft.
The data MUST hold ids, codes and instants only.
It MUST NOT hold a party id, a reference number, an answer, a name or an address.
Each event MUST also be a business event under OBS-070, whose main object is the file, under OBS-079.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Type |When
|`org.fpsot.applications.receipt.issued` |A submission or a reply created a receipt record.
|`org.fpsot.applications.file.changed` |A file moved to another state.
|`org.fpsot.applications.information-request.changed` |A request opened, was resolved, was cancelled or expired.
|`org.fpsot.applications.draft.migrated` |A draft moved to a newer form version.
|`org.fpsot.applications.draft.purged` |The service purged a draft.
|===

A case system, a fee engine or a register reads the file by its id, with its own authority.
