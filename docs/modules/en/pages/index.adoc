= Take in applications under the applications specification
:page-type: how-to
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This guide takes a public body from one program to a conforming intake.
Each step cites the rule that it meets.
It is not legal advice, and the page of questions for counsel lists the points that a lawyer must settle.
The files in `examples/` follow six synthetic applications through every step.

== Name the law

Find the profile whose law applies to the program, such as `ca-federal` or `ca-qc`, under APP-010.
If none fits, write one, and cite a source for each rule and each holiday, under APP-011.
Mark each source that nobody read at its origin as `to-be-verified`.
Keep every calendar, language rule and time of receipt in the profile, never in code, under APP-012.

== Declare the service

Write a service declaration with its forms, its clocks, its channels and its draft period.
State whether the program allows closing for no response, under APP-057, and self-declared submissions, under APP-072.
Offer a route that is not digital, under APP-093.
The file `examples/services/example-home-adaptation-grant.yaml` shows a Québec program with two clocks.

== Publish the form

Write each form as a JSON Schema 2020-12 document with a canonical address and a version, under APP-020.
Keep the id of each question while its meaning stays, and give a new id when it changes.
Never change an active version: publish a new one, under APP-021.
Give each question a label in each language, a purpose and a required flag, under APP-023 and APP-025.

== Keep drafts and take submissions

Keep drafts on the side of the service, and keep them past the end of a session, under APP-033.
Tell the applicant how long a draft lasts, and send a notice before a purge, under APP-034.
Show every answer before submission, then check the answers and refuse while a file is not clean, under APP-036.
Make submit idempotent, under APP-094.

== Record the arrival

Create a sealed receipt record for each submission, with the instant when it became accessible, under APP-040 to APP-042.
For paper, use the stamp on arrival, not the instant of keying, under APP-092.
A date stamp counts from the start of its local day, under APP-041.
Send an acknowledgment with the reference, the received time, the handling body and the expected date, under APP-043.
Draw each reference at random, with a Crockford check character, under APP-045.

== Ask for more

Open one request for information at a time, under APP-051.
Give it a reason, its items, a deadline and its effect on each clock, under APP-050.
Compute the deadline from the calendar of the profile, under APP-055.

Notify the applicant when it opens, under APP-084.
Decide nothing adverse while the request is open and in time, under APP-052.
After it ends, decide nothing adverse until the applicant has heard of it, under APP-052 and APP-053.
Close a file for no response only after an expired deadline and a separate, logged contact attempt, under APP-057.

== Handle files

Check each file by its content, hash it with SHA-256 only, and keep its original, under APP-061, APP-062 and APP-064.
Never use MD5 or SHA-1, not even as a checksum in transit.

== Show the status

Show one of seven labels, mapped from the state, under APP-080.
Show what is needed, by when and how, only with `action-needed`, under APP-081.
Keep notifications free of decisions and answers, and link only to your own domains, under APP-085 and APP-086.

== Test

Run `bash examples/test-examples.sh` after `pip install --require-hashes -r examples/requirements.txt`.
Write scenarios in English and in French, under APP-102, with synthetic data only, under APP-103.
